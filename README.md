# 📊 Online Shopping Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyse online shopping sales performance using transactional e-commerce data. The dashboard provides insights into customer purchasing behaviour, product category performance, revenue trends, and membership contributions to support business decision making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyse monthly sales performance and revenue trends
- Identify the best selling product categories
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour across different customer groups
- Compare online and offline spending patterns
- Evaluate revenue contribution by membership level
- Support data driven business decisions through interactive visualisations

## ❓ Business Questions

- Which product categories generate the highest sales?
- How do customer purchasing behaviours differ by gender?
- Which membership level contributes the most revenue?
- How do online and offline sales compare?
- What are the monthly sales trends throughout the year?

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Electronic Devices generated the highest overall sales.
- Gold members contributed the highest revenue among all membership levels.
- Offline purchases generated higher revenue compared to online purchases.
- Customer purchasing behaviour varied across product categories and gender.
- Monthly sales trends highlighted seasonal fluctuations in revenue.
